## I Knew Nothing About Astro, JavaScript...
As I looked into options for an environment to build this portfolio on I found 
out about GitHub Pages and loved the idea of having this window into my work
in the same site as the work itself. I began searching for guides on how to
make that possible and found a <Link variant={'inline'} href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v7HyyOnixJ8" text="video" newWindow />
that caught my eye *(probably because the title claimed I'd be **"done in 5
minutes"**, but I'm quite the perfectionist so that ended up not being at all
accurate)*. The video contemplated the option of using *Astro* and a premade
template, I thought that might be nice but I had a little big issue: **I'd have
to learn both *Astro* and *JavaScript* completely from scratch!**
### An Opportunity Surfaces
*"I've got a knack for this stuff"*, I thought, *"There's no way it's that difficult, right?"*
and went for it. But it wasn't long before the first issues became apparent:

## And Now What?
### Do I Know JavaScript?
**Not even close**, but it's not completely obscure to me anymore.
### Do I Know Astro?
**Kind of**. It has a ton of features by nature so in this little time
*(and not even with a lot more time on top of that)* there's no way to cover
everything — but just like with *JS*, I get by with a handful of things and
keep figuring out more as I go.
### Did I Create A Portfolio?
**Yeah, and I'm quite happy with how it turned out!**. Happy enough that,
beyond it being a good excuse to get familiar with *JavaScript*, I might
actually keep it updated. *(Or at least I'll try — knowing me, no idea how
long that'll last...)*
